# Design review

People expect what was reviewed, the screen next to the observation, and a decision. Keep this order unless the user wants a different one.

## How to fill it

- **Title** names the flow or surface and the decision needed.
- **Scope** says which screens or states are in this review, and which are not.
- **Observation slides** use the `split` template. Put the screenshot in the deck `assets/` folder and set that slide's `metadata.json` `image` to `assets/file.png`. The heading is the observation, not the screen name.
- **Issues** collect only what the room must resolve. Five bullets is the ceiling.
- **Recommendation** is one slide: what to change, and what you are asking the room to decide.
- **Closing** names the owner and the next check. Do not add a new critique there.
- One screenshot per split slide. Add or remove observation slides as a whole.
- Label assumptions. Replace every "Replace with" placeholder.
