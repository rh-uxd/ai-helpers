# Research readout

People expect the question, how you learned it, findings one at a time, and what should change. Keep this order unless the user wants a different one.

## How to fill it

- **Title** names the study and who it is for.
- **Question and method** stay on one content slide. Name the method and the scale ("6 interviews"), not a methods essay.
- **Findings** open with a section slide, then one content or quote slide per finding. Use the participant's words in a quote slide when the source has them.
- **Recommendations** say what should change and who it is for. Do not add a recommendation the study did not support.
- **Closing** is the decision you are asking for, or the next study question.
- Add or remove finding slides as a whole. Do not merge three findings onto one slide.
- Label assumptions. Replace every "Replace with" placeholder.
