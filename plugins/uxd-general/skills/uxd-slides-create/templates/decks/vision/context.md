# Vision

People expect a promise, the person and the work, the current path, the proposed path, one screen per step, and a before-and-after. Keep this order unless the user wants a different one.

## How to fill it

- **Agenda** is the cover. The heading is the promise. The subheading is one sentence. Each `###` is a step number (`01`) and the paragraph under it is the step name.
- **Persona** names one person in the subheading. Paragraphs under that are the role. `### Her goal` is the goal. `### 5 of 30` is a count; the paragraph under it is the caption, and the grid fills that many cells. The next `###` is the task, with numbered bullets. Later `###` headings are teams. A bullet may use `→` between two actions. `### Caption` is the small note and is not shown as a heading.
- **Stages** is four columns. A quote that starts with `Pain` makes the slide the current, gray version. A quote that starts with `Addresses` makes it the proposed version. `### Path`, `### Banner`, and `### Caption` are chrome, not columns. `####` is a label inside a column. A quote plus the next paragraph is a card.
- **Scene** puts the subheading and the first paragraph in the left callout. The first `###` is the console bar. A short paragraph under it, such as `Proposed interface`, sits on the right of that bar. `####` blocks are panels. A `####` title that contains `|` is a table for the panel above it. `#### Aside:` is the side panel. The first bold line in a panel is its title. A later bold line is a button. `[x]` bullets are checks. Numbered bullets are a history list. `### Caption` sits above the footer.
- **Compare** is two columns, `Today` then `Proposed`. A quote plus the next paragraph is a step. A quote that contains `Case submitted` is the closing bar.
- The footer series name comes from the deck theme. The page number is the slide order.
- Label assumptions. Replace every "Replace with" placeholder.
