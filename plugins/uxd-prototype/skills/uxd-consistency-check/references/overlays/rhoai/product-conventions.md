## Rule

Review navigation labels, terminology, and interaction choices against the
requester's versioned product guidance and nearby comparable workflows.
Confirm intended user roles and states before recommending convergence.

This is a product-specific example overlay — opt-in and not a general
requirement. This prose overlay is context, not an executable rule. The
deterministic analyzer must report it unevaluated rather than marking these
conventions passed. Do not infer additional product requirements or fetch
undocumented external sources from this example.
