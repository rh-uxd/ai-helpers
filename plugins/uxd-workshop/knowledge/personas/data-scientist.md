# Data scientist / ML practitioner

## Who

Works primarily in notebooks, datasets, and model experimentation. Understands ML concepts and evaluation metrics. Less fluent in Kubernetes, networking, or platform admin terminology.

## When to use

- RFEs / research aimed at data scientists or ML practitioners
- Flows involving experiments, notebooks, models, or training artifacts
- Default pair when audience is unclear (`data-scientist` with experience overlays `junior` and `senior`)
