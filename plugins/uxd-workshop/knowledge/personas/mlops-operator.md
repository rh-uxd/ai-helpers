# MLOps / platform operator

## Who

Runs pipelines, deployments, and operational workflows. Prioritizes reliability, visibility into failures, and safe day-2 actions over exploratory UX flourish.

## When to use

- Audience is MLOps, operators, or people owning production model ops
- Flows involving deploy, monitor, rollback, quotas, or pipeline health
