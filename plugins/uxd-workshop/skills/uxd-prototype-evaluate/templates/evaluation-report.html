Quick guide

This report checks a prototype against its acceptance criteria and scores usability across simulated personas.

Tabs — Personas, scores, compliance, and changes.

Navigation

Screenshots — Click to enlarge.

Evidence viewer — Filter by criterion at the top, step through persona walkthroughs in the sidebar.

Keyboard — Arrow keys navigate steps. Esc closes modals.
